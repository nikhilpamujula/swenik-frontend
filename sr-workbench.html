What this does

It watches Bitcoin's price and tells you when it reaches a price that has mattered before — and whether the evidence says it will hold or break.

✨ LevelsFinds the prices where the market has repeatedly turned around. It shows you candidates and their evidence; you choose which to keep.
⚡ ReadRuns the usual indicators — trend, momentum, Fibonacci, structure — and gives one combined verdict with a target.
🔗Only if you want the two above to work together. They stay separate otherwise.
✏️Draw your own lines. Long-press the chart for a crosshair and a menu.

The tabs

WATCHAll the coins you follow, scanned at once — which ones are near a level that matters.
SIGNALSThe only tab you need most days: is anything worth acting on right now?
TRADESPractice trades with fake money, tracked properly.
⚡ LIVEReal orders only — never mixed with practice. Position P&L, wallet margin, and every amendment the manager makes or proposes.
COACHAfter a few trades, it tells you which of your own habits cost you money.
RECORDChecks whether this app's percentages are actually honest. Run the backtest there.

It will often say "nothing actionable". That is the app working, not failing — most of the time there genuinely is no good trade.
